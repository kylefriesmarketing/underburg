# Ünderburg — Empires of the Deep

[Play Ünderburg](https://kylefriesmarketing.github.io/underburg/)

A 3D underwater fortress roguelite. Sail between sea-chart tiles, salvage gold, collect artifacts, upgrade your submarine citadel, and defeat the guardians of fifteen original missions.

No installation or account required. Use a modern browser with WebGL. Progress saves in the browser on your own device.

**Controls:** WASD/arrows steer; Shift boosts; Space emits sonar; Q/E/R/F/C/V trigger abilities; X fires all ready abilities; B opens upgrades. On the sea chart, drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and select a tile to choose a mission. Touch controls are included.

Original Blender models and synthesized audio. Third-party library notices are in [LICENSES.md](LICENSES.md).
